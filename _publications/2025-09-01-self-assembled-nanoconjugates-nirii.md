**First author.** A polymer–drug nanoconjugate (OPGAX) that combines NIR-II aggregation-induced emission with transcytosis-driven tumor penetration. D–A charge separation was validated by DFT; the platform outperformed IR-26 and ICG as a NIR-II fluorophore and achieved 75.7% tumor inhibition in 4T1 tumor-bearing mice, superior to paclitaxel.
