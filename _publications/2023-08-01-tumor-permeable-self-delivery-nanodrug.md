**First author.** Designed and synthesized the amphiphilic prodrug CPT-S-NO that self-assembles into a ROS-responsive, self-delivery system; demonstrated longer blood half-life, higher tumor accumulation and deeper tumor penetration than the clinical parent drug, with mitochondrial targeting confirmed by confocal imaging and HPLC.
