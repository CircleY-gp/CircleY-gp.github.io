**First author.** Built an OPGA-PTX conjugate library (varying hydrophilic–hydrophobic ratio and oxidized tertiary amine content) via NCA ring-opening polymerization, resolved endocytic pathways and subcellular sorting, and clarified how polymer composition regulates Golgi-mediated transcytosis versus lysosomal drug release.
