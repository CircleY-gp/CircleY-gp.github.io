I am a Ph.D. candidate in **Chemistry** at [The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)](https://hkust.edu.hk), where I work on the development of **AIE-based functional materials for integrated phototheranostics** — spanning small molecules, polymers, drug delivery and biotherapy.

My research combines conjugated molecular design, self-assembly and biomedical evaluation: I build heavy-atom-free aggregation-induced emission (AIE) systems that synchronize fluorescence emission and reactive oxygen species (ROS) generation, and polymer–drug nanoconjugates that exploit **transcytosis** for deep tumour penetration, NIR-II imaging and activatable therapy.

Before joining HKUST, I received my **M.Sc.Eng. in Biochemical Engineering** from Zhejiang University (2020–2023), where I worked on antitumor nanomedicine based on polymers, nanomaterials and prodrug self-assembly, and my **B.Sc.Eng. in Pharmaceutical Engineering** from Sichuan University (2016–2020), graduating with a GPA of 3.75/4 (top 8%).

## Research Interests

- Aggregation-induced emission (AIE) luminogens and phototheranostics
- Photodynamic/photothermal therapy and NIR-II fluorescence imaging
- Polymer–drug conjugates, prodrug nanomedicines and stimuli-responsive delivery
- Transcytosis-mediated tumour penetration and subcellular drug transport
- Structure–property relationships guided by DFT calculations
