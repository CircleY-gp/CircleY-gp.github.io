Research and project experience
======
* **Heavy-atom-free pyridine-based AIEgens for enhanced phototheranostics** (Aug 2025 – present)
  * **Molecular design & synthesis**: constructed heavy-atom-free pyridine-based AIEgens achieving the first synergistic enhancement of ROS generation and fluorescence emission; obtained uniform 64 nm nanoparticles via solvent-regulated self-assembly.
  * **Photophysical characterization**: revealed the multi-level ISC mechanism by steady-state/transient spectroscopy and DFT calculations; achieved 10-fold enhanced aggregation emission, 800-fold higher PLQY and 1200-fold faster ROS generation within 60 s.
  * **Antitumor evaluation**: confirmed in vitro phototoxicity with IC50 < 1 µM, superior to analogous molecules; elucidated transcellular transport by confocal microscopy and flow cytometry, enabling synergistic photodynamic and transcytosis-mediated therapy; preliminary in vivo results confirmed imaging capability and effective tumor-targeting enrichment.

* **Self-assembled nanoconjugates with aggregation-induced emission for NIR-II imaging and transcytosis-driven cancer therapy** (Oct 2023 – Aug 2025)
  * **Material design**: synthesized OPGAX, a poly[L-γ-[2-(N-oxide-N,N-dimethylamino)ethyl]glutamine]-paclitaxel for NIR-II imaging and long circulation; uniform nanoparticles (< 100 nm, 42.5% drug loading) via self-assembly optimization.
  * **Optical characterization**: validated D–A charge separation by DFT; > 35-fold enhanced aggregation emission and 0.549% NIR-II quantum yield, outperforming IR-26 and ICG.
  * **Efficacy evaluation**: 89.5% drug release at 72 h; prolonged circulation and 48 h tumor enrichment in vivo; 75.7% tumor inhibition in 4T1 mice, superior to paclitaxel.
  * **Publication**: *Angew. Chem. Int. Ed.* (IF 16.9).

* **Tumor-permeable self-delivery nanodrug targeting mitochondria for enhanced chemotherapy** (Aug 2022 – Jan 2023)
  * **Prodrug design & synthesis**: synthesized the amphiphilic prodrug CPT-S-NO (oxidized tertiary amine–thioether–camptothecin), which self-assembled into a ROS-responsive nanodelivery system for on-demand release.
  * **Distribution & penetration**: showed longer blood half-life, higher tumor accumulation and deeper penetration than its clinical parent drug by in vivo imaging and isolation-based detection.
  * **Targeting & release**: confirmed mitochondrial targeting and ROS-responsive release by confocal imaging and HPLC; elucidated antitumor action via mitochondrial damage and transcytosis.
  * **Publication & patent**: *J. Control. Release* (IF 13.1); patent CN202311070730.4.

* **Modulating intracellular dynamics for optimized intracellular release and transcytosis equilibrium** (Mar 2021 – Aug 2022)
  * **Material library & screening**: synthesized OPGA-PTX conjugates with varying hydrophilic–hydrophobic ratio (HHR) and oxidized tertiary amine ratios (TP) via NCA ring-opening polymerization; optimized formulation OPPX (HHR = 10:1, TP = 100%).
  * **Intracellular transport & subcellular distribution**: analyzed endocytic pathways and subcellular sorting of different formulations; elucidated how polymer hydrophilic–hydrophobic composition regulates Golgi-mediated transcytosis and lysosomal drug release.
  * **Tumor penetration & efficacy**: confirmed deep tumor penetration, tumor accumulation and synergistic active penetration/enzyme-responsive release in vitro and in vivo.
  * **Publication**: *Adv. Mater.* (IF 29.6).
