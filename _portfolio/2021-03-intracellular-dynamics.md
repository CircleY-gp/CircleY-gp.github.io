**Role:** Master's project · Zhejiang University · Mar 2021 – Aug 2022

- Synthesized OPGA-PTX conjugates with varying hydrophilic–hydrophobic ratio (HHR) and oxidized tertiary amine ratios (TP) via NCA ring-opening polymerization; optimized the formulation OPPX (HHR = 10:1, TP = 100%).
- Analyzed endocytic pathways and subcellular sorting of different formulations; elucidated how polymer hydrophilic–hydrophobic composition regulates Golgi-mediated transcytosis and lysosomal drug release.
- Confirmed deep tumor penetration, tumor accumulation and synergistic active penetration/enzyme-responsive release in vitro and in vivo. Published in *Adv. Mater.* (2024).
