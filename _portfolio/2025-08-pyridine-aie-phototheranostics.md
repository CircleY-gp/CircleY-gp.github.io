**Role:** Ph.D. project · HKUST · Aug 2025 – present

- Constructed heavy-atom-free pyridine-based AIEgens, achieving the first synergistic enhancement of ROS generation and fluorescence emission; obtained uniform 64 nm nanoparticles via solvent-regulated self-assembly.
- Revealed the multi-level intersystem crossing (ISC) mechanism by steady-state/transient spectroscopy and DFT calculations; achieved 10-fold enhanced aggregation emission, 800-fold higher PLQY and 1200-fold faster ROS generation within 60 s.
- Confirmed in vitro phototoxicity with IC50 < 1 µM, superior to analogous molecules; elucidated transcellular transport by confocal microscopy and flow cytometry, enabling synergistic photodynamic and transcytosis-mediated therapy; preliminary in vivo results confirmed imaging capability and effective tumor-targeting enrichment.
