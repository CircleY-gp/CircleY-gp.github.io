**Role:** Research project · HKUST / Zhejiang University · Oct 2023 – Aug 2025

- Synthesized OPGAX, a poly[L-γ-[2-(N-oxide-N,N-dimethylamino)ethyl]glutamine]-paclitaxel conjugate for NIR-II imaging and long circulation; uniform nanoparticles (< 100 nm, 42.5% drug loading) through self-assembly optimization.
- Validated D–A charge separation by DFT; > 35-fold enhanced aggregation emission and 0.549% NIR-II quantum yield, outperforming IR-26 and ICG.
- 89.5% drug release at 72 h; prolonged circulation and 48 h tumor enrichment in vivo; 75.7% tumor inhibition in 4T1 mice, superior to paclitaxel. Published in *Angew. Chem. Int. Ed.* (2025).
