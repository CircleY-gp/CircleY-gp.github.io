**Role:** Research project · Zhejiang University · Aug 2022 – Jan 2023

- Synthesized the amphiphilic prodrug CPT-S-NO (oxidized tertiary amine–thioether–camptothecin), which self-assembles into a ROS-responsive nanodelivery system for on-demand release.
- Showed longer blood half-life, higher tumor accumulation and deeper penetration than its clinical parent drug by in vivo imaging and isolation-based detection.
- Confirmed mitochondrial targeting and ROS-responsive release by confocal imaging and HPLC; elucidated the antitumor mechanism via mitochondrial damage and transcytosis.
- Output: *J. Control. Release* (IF 13.1); patent CN202311070730.4.
